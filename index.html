MY 2025-2026 YEAR IN REVIEW (JUNIOR)

At the start of my Junior year, I entered the CART Web App program with about a 30% comfort level. I assumed the whole class would be about coding 24/7, but I knew myself that I wasn’t the very best at coding. At this point, the only thing fueling my “coding career” was my childhood dream to create a game. The first project I was given, my head felt numb from the immense amount of troubleshooting and code conflicts that were thrown at me. I remember seeing the blank VS Code screen, and thinking in my head “How the H*ll am I even supposed to start.” Before taking the class, I would frequently use coding sites that gave me that same blank look. Every single time I would watch a video tutorial, but of course they all were short-lived. However during an HTML + CSS project called “CSS Zen Garden” was assigned to me, that’s when my prowess began to show. Although I didn’t get HTML5 and CSS3 immediately, the more I coded, the more efficient I got. Every time I was given a roadblock that I overcame, I would gain confidence in coding more and more. Many Habits of Mind helped me in my coding journey, but the ones that influenced me the most was communication and collaboration. I definitely wouldn’t be as good of a coder without the help of my peers.

However, once semester 2 came around the corner, I started to use more complex coding languages and tools. Some of which include animations, and JavaScript. This Portfolio emphasizes the journey I went through during my junior year at CART. It not only provided me with knowledge with coding, but it also gave me professional behavior that I can exercise in a competitive career field.
