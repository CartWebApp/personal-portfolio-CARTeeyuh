ATTENTION ECONOMY ARGUMENTATIVE ESSAY

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Brief 2-3 Day Essay outlining the difference in “power” over the attention economy regarding social media. It highlights how attention seekers (aka those who control the algorithm) hold significantly more power over attention givers (users, people who utilize social media)  because the givers don’t seem to do anything to fix the imbalance.

Concepts Learned

Elusive Flow:
Throughout the entire essay, I was told by my peers that it had an “ever-elusive flow” and that it was hard to grasp, luring them to keep reading it until they did catch it. They never did.

Quick Thinking:
The essay was told to be finished in a relatively short time frame (Approx~ 3 days) for an essay.

Strong Voice:Utilized wording that made my speech have an extra punch to it. For example, by metaphorically comparing my essay as a sword that must be “wielded”.

Challenge 1

Only allowed to write on the essay during class hours, meaning I only have a ~3 hour window of typing.

Solution 1

Read and annotated my sources outside of school, allowing me to use the whole 3 hours specifically for writing, and not wasting it reading the sources.

Challenge 2

Struggled to create the correct voice that I wanted my essay to portray.

Solution 2

As I was writing, I would speak in a strong voice that I wanted my essay to portray.

Challenge 3

Lack of peer review due to a low amount of time.

Challenge 3

Requested AI to read my essay, to check any grammar mistakes, and gave me pointers that may aid in how I wanted my essay to sound like.

PERSISTENCE

Outside of school, instead of waiting for tomorrow before I can contribute to the essay, I started to read and annotate the sources. This allowed me to be absolutely ready for my next window of typing.

INITIATE

Did not sit around and wait, but instead I went out of my way to do as much as I can in this assignment, despite any circumstances given to me.
