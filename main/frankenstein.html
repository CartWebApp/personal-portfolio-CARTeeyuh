Frankenstein Constructed Response

Project Overview

A brief 1 paragraph assignment that highlights the idea of who should deserve more empathy to, Victor Frankenstein, or the Monster he created.

Concepts Learned

Quick Thinking:
Had to think very swiftly, but carefully, due to my very limited time (Approx~ 1 hour).

Patter Recognition:
Followed a pattern that is named "RACED", stands for Restate, Answer, Cite, Evidence, Drop the mic.

Challenges and Solutions

    Challenge 1

    Low amount of time to write the essay.

    Solution 1

    Read the sources given to me beforehand to have a quicker thinking process.

    Challenge 2

    Hard to choose who was more deserving of Empathy.

    Solution 2

    Had to list out all the actions that both characters did, as well as what has happened to them, pinpointing where on the moral scale they land.

    Challenge 3

    Difficult to find the voice I wanted to portray.

    Solution 3

    Voiced out what I wanted the listeners to hear, and argue on why I believed my standpoint.

Investigate

I read the book "Frankenstein" beforehand, and made sure to understand what each character did and how I interpreted their actions.

Reflect

Reflected upon the book and how I understood their actions, whether they were morally correct or wrong.
