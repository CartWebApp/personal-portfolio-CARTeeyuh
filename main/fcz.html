FCZ App

Description

An app to be used by Fresno Chaffee Zoo Care Specialists (more commonly known as “zookeepers”) that allows them to manage their enrichment activities. It is a more accessible way for ZCS to do their job, ultimately making their life/job easier.

The Technology Stack

    Languages: HTML, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript

    Tools: Visual Studio Code for development and Git/GitHub for version control

    Deployment: Hosted via Netlify to ensure global accessibility and performance.

Challenges & Solutions

    Challenge 1

    A large group of people means problems may arise through communication and collaboration.

    Solution 1

    Established a clear leader that led us through the project, and a strong team that would all do the assignment given to them.

    Challenge 2

    Several groups all doing their own separate thing may arise connectivity issues when implementing them all in the end.

    Solution 2

    Frequent communication between groups.

    Challenge 3

    User flow was tacky, unintuitive

    Solution 3

    Underwent a case study that pinpointed the specific abnormalities within the user flow.

Reflect

We frequently went back to old versions of the App to know what had to be done, and to see if we have already solved that problem.

Communication

Me and my peers would frequently check up on each other, just to make sure we are caught up in our assignments.
